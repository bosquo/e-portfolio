About Me

Connecting forestry engineering and a practical technical foundation with modern geoinformatics, GeoAI, and 3D spatial technologies.

Florian Herbst

Salzburg, Austria · University of Salzburg (Z_GIS)

I graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Forestry Engineering in Germany, and I am currently specializing in GIS in Austria, where I study Applied Geoinformatics (MSc). I have professional experience in both the GIS domain and in the forestry industry, building on a technical foundation, as I started my career as an Electrician, specializing in energy and building technology.

In my current work and studies at the University of Salzburg I focus on working with cutting edge technologies such as GeoAI, 3D GIS, Digital Twinning, Photogrammetry, and Gaussian Splatting.

Currently, I am part of UNIGIS Salzburg, a distance learning program for GIS professionals. My work there primarily involves curating learning materials and lectures for a global audience of students. This role allows me to learn, research, and teach technologies and methods at the cutting edge of GIS.

Generally, my interests lie in 3D, Digital Twinning, and GeoAI.
